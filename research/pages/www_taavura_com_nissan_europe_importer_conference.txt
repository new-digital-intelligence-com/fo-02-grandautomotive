Nissan Europe Importer Conference
This week in Paris, Grand Automotive Central Europe was recognized at the annual Nissan Europe Importer Conference for achieving the highest sales growth among all 18 Nissan European importer markets during the 2025 financial year.
The award was accepted by Viktor Molnar on behalf of Grand Automotive Central Europe .
Receiving this recognition for the 3. consecutive year makes this achievement even more meaningful for our entire organization.
Today, GACE operates across 12 countries and represents nearly 25% of Nissan’s total European importer market volume, a responsibility we approach with ambition, consistency and long-term commitment.
The award was presented by Nissan Europe leadership:
• Massimiliano Messina
• Jordi Vila
• Andrew Humberstone
• Olivier Cavarec
This achievement reflects the dedication of our colleagues, dealer partners, customers and Nissan Europe teams across the region.
Thank you to everyone who contributes to this shared success. 🫶🙏
At the same time, we are already looking ahead.
Our new 10-week campaign has officially started with one of the strongest offers of the year and we are fully motivated to continue raising the bar in 2026 as well. 🚀
