Tracking Technologies and Cookies
At Grand Automotive, we may utilize tracking technologies, such as cookies, to enhance your experience on our website and to analyze how our website is used. Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences, track your interactions with our website, and improve our services.
Types of Cookies Used
Session Cookies: Session cookies are temporary cookies that are erased when you close your browser. These cookies are essential for the proper functioning of our website, enabling you to navigate between pages and access secure areas.
Persistent Cookies: Persistent cookies remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them. These cookies help us recognize you as a returning visitor and personalize your experience on our website.
Third-Party Cookies: We may also use third-party cookies provided by trusted partners to analyze website traffic, track user interactions, and improve our services. These cookies may collect information about your browsing behavior on our website and other websites you visit.
Purpose of Cookies
We use cookies for the following purposes:
Functionality: Cookies help us provide you with personalized content, remember your preferences, and enhance your user experience.
Analytics: We use cookies to analyze how visitors interact with our website, identify areas for improvement, and measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.
Advertising: We may use cookies to deliver targeted advertisements based on your interests and browsing behavior, both on our website and on third-party websites.
Cookie Consent
By continuing to use our website, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this Privacy Policy. You can manage your cookie preferences and opt-out of certain types of cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website and your ability to access certain features.
Third-Party Tracking
Some third-party services we use, such as Google Analytics, may also place cookies on your device to track your interactions with our website and provide us with analytics and insights. These third-party cookies are subject to the privacy policies of the respective providers.
Your Choices
You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most web browsers allow you to accept, decline, or delete cookies. You can also opt-out of certain types of cookies by adjusting your browser settings or using third-party opt-out tools.
