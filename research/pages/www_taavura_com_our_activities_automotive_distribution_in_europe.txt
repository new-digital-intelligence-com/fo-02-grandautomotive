Automotive Distribution & Retail in Europe
Grand Automotive provides comprehensive mobility services across the entire automotive spectrum in Central and Eastern Europe. As a leading distributor of passenger cars, light commercial vehicles and spare parts, Grand Automotive also manages retail sites offering sales and service, financing solutions, automotive insurance, and professional used car sales through its regional subsidiary, complemented by an extensive range of related automotive services.

Established in 2002 and part of the Taavura Holdings Group, Grand Automotive operates across 15 dynamic markets: Hungary, Czech Republic, Slovakia, Slovenia, Croatia, Serbia, North Macedonia, Bosnia Herzegovina, Albania, Montenegro, Kosovo, Bulgaria, Greece, Cyprus, and Austria.
Leveraging a robust network of over 300 independent dealerships and 26 strategically located company-owned showrooms, Grand Automotive guarantees exceptional market coverage and a superior customer experience.
Representing renowned global brands such as Ford, Hyundai, Nissan, Renault, Dacia, Alpine, MG, Chery, Omoda, Jaecoo, Ineos, Maxus, Piaggio, and additional premium retail brands, Grand Automotive's diverse portfolio caters to a broad spectrum of customer preferences and needs.
Through ongoing collaboration with the Group’s Automotive Innovation Center in Israel, Grand Automotive continuously brings advanced automotive technologies and mobility innovations to its markets, actively shaping Europe's automotive future.
Selling approximately 70,000 vehicles annually, Grand Automotive stands as a key and trusted player in Europe's automotive industry, dedicated to excellence, innovation, and unmatched customer satisfaction.
